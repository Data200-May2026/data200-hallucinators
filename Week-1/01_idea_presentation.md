## Project Topic

**Selected Topic:** Nepal Nutrition Indicators - Statistical Analysis of Child Health Metrics

## Problem Statement

Child malnutrition remains a critical public health concern in Nepal. Using WHO Global Health Observatory data, we will analyze trends in key nutrition indicators (stunting, wasting, underweight, breastfeeding practices) to identify patterns, demographic disparities, and statistical relationships that can inform public health interventions.
